
## About

Articles, _ein/kein_, and adjective endings. The example nouns are **der Mann, das Kind, die Frau, die Leute**, and the example adjective is **alt**.

---

## 0. General notes

### The core idea

Every noun phrase has to show its **gender, number and case** somewhere. German puts this signal on the article. When the article can't show it, the adjective does. Almost every rule in this document follows from that.

### The four cases and when to use them

|Case|Job in the sentence|Question|Example|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Nominativ**|Subject|_Wer? Was?_|**Der Mann** schläft.|
|**Akkusativ**|Direct object|_Wen? Was?_|Ich sehe **den Mann**.|
|**Dativ**|Indirect object (recipient)|_Wem?_|Ich gebe **dem Mann** das Buch.|
|**Genitiv**|Possession ("of")|_Wessen?_|Das Auto **des Mannes**.|

### Patterns worth noticing

- **Only the masculine changes in the Akkusativ.** Neuter, feminine and plural Akkusativ always look the same as their Nominativ.
- **Masculine and neuter are the same in the Dativ and Genitiv.**
- **Feminine and plural are the same in the Nominativ and Akkusativ** (_die_).
- **Feminine nouns never change their ending in the singular.**
- **The Dativ plural always ends in -n**, on the article, the adjective and the noun: _den alt**en** Leute**n**_.
- **The Genitiv is fading** in everyday speech. People often say _von dem_ instead (_das Auto vom Mann_). You still need to recognize it, and it's used in writing.

## 1. Definite article

|Kasus|Maskulin|Neutrum|Feminin|Plural|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Nom**|der Mann|das Kind|die Frau|die Leute|
|**Akk**|**den** Mann|das Kind|die Frau|die Leute|
|**Dat**|**dem** Mann|**dem** Kind|**der** Frau|**den** Leute**n**|
|**Gen**|**des** Mann**es**|**des** Kind**es**|**der** Frau|**der** Leute|

**Notes**

- **Genitiv masculine/neuter:** The noun takes **-(e)s**. Short one-syllable nouns usually take _-es_ (_des Mannes_), and longer ones take _-s_ (_des Vaters, des Lehrers_).
- **Dativ plural -n:** Not added if the plural already ends in _-n_ or _-s_ (_den Frauen, den Autos_).
- **Mnemonic for the endings:** **r-s-e-e / n-s-e-e / m-m-r-n / s-s-r-r**, read row by row. Nom = de**r** da**s** di**e** di**e**, and so on.
- **Other words that work like _der_:** _dieser, jener, jeder, welcher, mancher, solcher, alle_ (plural). They all take the same endings: _dies**er** Mann, dies**en** Mann, dies**em** Kind, dies**es** Mannes_.

**Common contractions** (preposition + article): _an dem → **am**, in dem → **im**, bei dem → **beim**, von dem → **vom**, zu dem → **zum**, zu der → **zur**, in das → **ins**, an das → **ans**_

## 2. Indefinite article and _kein_

|Kasus|Maskulin|Neutrum|Feminin|Plural|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Nom**|(k)ein Mann|(k)ein Kind|(k)ein**e** Frau|kein**e** Leute|
|**Akk**|(k)ein**en** Mann|(k)ein Kind|(k)ein**e** Frau|kein**e** Leute|
|**Dat**|(k)ein**em** Mann|(k)ein**em** Kind|(k)ein**er** Frau|kein**en** Leute**n**|
|**Gen**|(k)ein**es** Mann**es**|(k)ein**es** Kind**es**|(k)ein**er** Frau|kein**er** Leute|

**Notes**

- **kein = ein + k** in the singular. _ein_ has **no plural** (_Ich sehe Leute_), but _kein_ does.
- **Three cells have no ending:** masculine Nominativ, neuter Nominativ and neuter Akkusativ. These are the only places where _ein_ doesn't show gender, and they matter a lot for adjectives (section 5).
- **All the other endings match the last letter(s) of der/die/das:** de**n** → ein**en**, de**m** → ein**em**, de**r** → ein**er**, de**s** → ein**es**, di**e** → ein**e**.
- **The possessives work the same way (the "ein-words"):** _mein, dein, sein, ihr, unser, euer, Ihr_. For example: _mein Mann, mein**e** Frau, mein**em** Kind, mein**e** Leute_.
    - _euer_ drops its _e_ when it takes an ending: _eu**r**e Frau, eu**r**em Kind_.
- **_kein_ vs _nicht_:** Use _kein_ to negate a noun that would have _ein_ or no article (_Ich habe **kein** Auto_). Use _nicht_ to negate a noun with _der_ (_Ich sehe den Mann **nicht**_).

## 3. Adjective + noun, no article (strong declension)

|Kasus|Maskulin|Neutrum|Feminin|Plural|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Nom**|alt**er** Mann|alt**es** Kind|alt**e** Frau|alt**e** Leute|
|**Akk**|alt**en** Mann|alt**es** Kind|alt**e** Frau|alt**e** Leute|
|**Dat**|alt**em** Mann|alt**em** Kind|alt**er** Frau|alt**en** Leute**n**|
|**Gen**|alt**en** Mann**es**|alt**en** Kind**es**|alt**er** Frau|alt**er** Leute|

**Notes**

- **No article, so the adjective takes over its job.** The endings are the last letters of der/das/die/dem/den/der/des.
- **The one exception is masculine and neuter Genitiv:** the adjective takes **-en** instead of _-es_, because the noun's _-(e)s_ already shows the case. Rare in practice anyway.
- **When you see it:**
    - Uncountable nouns: _kalt**es** Wasser, mit frisch**er** Milch_
    - Plurals with no article: _alt**e** Leute_
    - After numbers and _viele, einige, mehrere, wenige_: _zwei alt**e** Leute, viele gut**e** Ideen_
    - Greetings: _Gut**en** Morgen, Lieb**er** Hans_

## 4. Definite article + adjective + noun (weak declension)

|Kasus|Maskulin|Neutrum|Feminin|Plural|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Nom**|der alt**e** Mann|das alt**e** Kind|die alt**e** Frau|die alt**en** Leute|
|**Akk**|den alt**en** Mann|das alt**e** Kind|die alt**e** Frau|die alt**en** Leute|
|**Dat**|dem alt**en** Mann|dem alt**en** Kind|der alt**en** Frau|den alt**en** Leute**n**|
|**Gen**|des alt**en** Mann**es**|des alt**en** Kind**es**|der alt**en** Frau|der alt**en** Leute|

**Notes**

- **Only two endings: -e and -en.** The article already shows everything, so the adjective has nothing to add.
- **The 5 "-e" cells form an L shape:** all three Nominativ singulars, plus neuter and feminine Akkusativ. All of these look exactly like their Nominativ.
- **Everything else is -en:** all plurals, all Dativ, all Genitiv, and masculine Akkusativ.
- **The same applies after every der-word:** _dieser, jeder, welcher, alle_. For example: _dieser alt**e** Mann, alle alt**en** Leute_.

## 5. _ein_/_kein_ + adjective + noun (mixed declension)

|Kasus|Maskulin|Neutrum|Feminin|Plural|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Nom**|ein alt**er** Mann|ein alt**es** Kind|eine alt**e** Frau|keine alt**en** Leute|
|**Akk**|einen alt**en** Mann|ein alt**es** Kind|eine alt**e** Frau|keine alt**en** Leute|
|**Dat**|einem alt**en** Mann|einem alt**en** Kind|einer alt**en** Frau|keinen alt**en** Leute**n**|
|**Gen**|eines alt**en** Mann**es**|eines alt**en** Kind**es**|einer alt**en** Frau|keiner alt**en** Leute|

**Notes**

- **This is table 4 with three exceptions.** Those are the three cells where _ein_ has no ending (section 2): masculine Nominativ, neuter Nominativ and neuter Akkusativ. There the adjective shows the gender instead: _ein alt**er** Mann, ein alt**es** Kind_.
- **The same applies after all possessives:** _mein alt**er** Freund, unser neu**es** Haus, meine alt**en** Freunde_.

## 6. Side-by-side comparison of adjective endings

Each cell shows **no article / der / ein**.

| Kasus   | Maskulin           | Neutrum            | Feminin         | Plural (no article / der / kein) |
| ------- | ------------------ | ------------------ | --------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Nom** | -er / -e / **-er** | -es / -e / **-es** | -e / -e / -e    | -e / -en / -en                   |
| **Akk** | -en / -en / -en    | -es / -e / **-es** | -e / -e / -e    | -e / -en / -en                   |
| **Dat** | -em / -en / -en    | -em / -en / -en    | -er / -en / -en | -en / -en / -en                  |
| **Gen** | -en / -en / -en    | -en / -en / -en    | -er / -en / -en | -er / -en / -en                  |

**What this shows**

- **The feminine singular is the same in all three systems in the Nominativ and Akkusativ** (_-e_).
- **The Dativ plural is always _-en_.**
- **The _ein_ column matches the no-article column in the 3 bold cells** and the _der_ column everywhere else.
- **Masculine Akkusativ is always _-en_,** whatever comes before it.

### One rule to rule them all

> **Is the signal (gender + case) already on the article?**
> 
> - **Yes** (_der, dem, einem, ..._) → the adjective takes **-e** (in the 5 Nominativ-like singular cells) or **-en** (everywhere else).
> - **No** (there's no article, or it's _ein_ with no ending) → the adjective takes the **strong ending**, which looks like the end of _der/das/die/dem_.

## 7. Notes by case: what triggers it

### Nominativ

- The subject: _**Der alte Mann** liest._
- After _sein, werden, bleiben_ (the predicate noun): _Er ist **ein guter Lehrer**._
- Predicate adjectives take **no ending**: _Der Mann ist **alt**._ Endings only appear when the adjective comes before the noun.

### Akkusativ

- The direct object: _Ich kenne **den alten Mann**._
- **Prepositions that always take the Akkusativ:** _durch, für, gegen, ohne, um, bis, entlang (after the noun)_. Mnemonic: **DOGFU** (durch, ohne, gegen, für, um).
- Time expressions with no preposition: _jed**en** Tag, nächst**en** Montag, d**en** ganz**en** Abend_.
- After _es gibt_: _Es gibt **einen** Park._

### Dativ

- The indirect object (usually a person): _Ich gebe **dem Kind** einen Apfel._
- **Prepositions that always take the Dativ:** _aus, bei, mit, nach, seit, von, zu, gegenüber_. You can sing them to the tune of _The Blue Danube_: "aus-bei-mit-nach-seit-von-zu".
- **Common verbs that take the Dativ:** _helfen, danken, gefallen, gehören, antworten, folgen, gratulieren, schmecken, passen, fehlen_. Example: _Ich helfe **der alten Frau**._
- **Many adjectives and expressions:** _Mir ist kalt. Das ist **mir** egal._

### Akkusativ or Dativ: two-way prepositions (_Wechselpräpositionen_)

_an, auf, hinter, in, neben, über, unter, vor, zwischen_

- **Movement toward a destination (_Wohin?_) → Akkusativ:** _Ich gehe **in den** Park._
- **Location (_Wo?_) → Dativ:** _Ich bin **im** (= in dem) Park._

### Genitiv

- Possession: _das Haus **des alten Mannes**, die Tasche **der Frau**._
- With names, add _-s_ and no article: _Peter**s** Auto_.
- **Prepositions:** _wegen, trotz, während, (an)statt, innerhalb, außerhalb_. In speech they often take the Dativ instead: _wegen **dem** Wetter_.

## 8. Watch out: n-nouns (_n-Deklination_)

Some masculine nouns add **-(e)n in every case except the Nominativ singular**:

|Singular|Plural|
|---|---|---|
|**Nom**|der Junge|die Junge**n**|
|**Akk**|den Junge**n**|die Junge**n**|
|**Dat**|dem Junge**n**|den Junge**n**|
|**Gen**|des Junge**n**|der Junge**n**|

- **Typical n-nouns:**
    - masculine nouns ending in _-e_ (_der Junge, Kollege, Kunde, Name_*)
    - masculine nouns ending in _-ent, -ant, -ist, -at_ (_der Student, Praktikant, Journalist, Soldat_)
    - a few others: _der Mensch, Herr (→ Herrn), Nachbar, Bär, Held_
- *_Name, Gedanke, Glaube_ take _-ns_ in the Genitiv: _des Namens_.
- _Mann_ is **not** an n-noun.

## 9. Learning tips

1. **Learn every noun with its article and plural:** _der Mann, -"er_. Without the gender, none of these tables can help you.
2. **Memorize the definite article table first.** Everything else builds on it:
    - The ein-endings are the article's last letters.
    - The strong adjective endings are also the article's last letters.
    - The weak adjective endings are just _-e_ and _-en_.
3. **Learn the shape, not 48 separate cells:**
    - Nominativ = Akkusativ except for the masculine.
    - Masculine = neuter in the Dativ and Genitiv.
    - The Dativ plural always ends in _-n_.
    - The weak declension has an L of _-e_ and _-en_ everywhere else.
4. **Drill short chunks out loud:** _mit dem alten Mann, mit einem alten Mann, mit altem Wein_. Hearing the ending that sounds right builds faster than recalling a rule.
5. **Ask yourself two questions every time:** _Which case?_ (verb or preposition) → _Has the article already shown it?_ (strong or weak ending)
6. **Colour-code your vocabulary by gender,** for example blue = der, green = das, red = die.

### Quick self-test (answers below)

1. Ich helfe ein___ alt___ Frau.
2. Wir fahren mit d___ neu___ Auto.
3. Das ist ein___ gut___ Idee.
4. Er trinkt kalt___ Wasser.
5. Ich habe kein___ klein___ Kinder.
6. Der Hund gehört d___ jung___ Mann.
7. Sie kauft ein___ neu___ Tisch.
8. Trotz d___ schlecht___ Wetters gehen wir raus.

### Answers

1. ein**er** alt**en** Frau (Dativ, feminine)
2. d**em** neu**en** Auto (Dativ, neuter)
3. ein**e** gut**e** Idee (Nominativ, feminine)
4. kalt**es** Wasser (Akkusativ, neuter, no article → strong)
5. kein**e** klein**en** Kinder (Akkusativ, plural)
6. d**em** jung**en** Mann (Dativ, masculine, _gehören_ + Dativ)
7. ein**en** neu**en** Tisch (Akkusativ, masculine)
8. d**es** schlecht**en** Wetters (Genitiv, neuter)
